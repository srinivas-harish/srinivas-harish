Srinivas Harish is a machine cognition researcher and the Chief Technology Officer at PREDICTION. He previously built perception and inference systems at Dockware and NVIDIA.

He currently works on reasoning and memory in language models, specifically how learned systems form, keep, change and act on internal state. That covers what a model holds in its activations, whether that state can live outside the model and be reused, combined and revised, and whether a model can reason in its own hidden states without passing its thinking through text, and how that reasoning can still be read. His long-term aim is to couple human creativity and judgment to machine memory, breadth and parallel inference. His earlier work was in evaluation, measuring what models can actually do, as opposed to what benchmarks credit them with, across language models and world models. His work has received a best paper award at ECCV 2026 OpenSUN3D.
