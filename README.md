Srinivas Harish is a machine cognition researcher and the Chief Technology Officer at PREDICTION. He previously built perception and inference systems at Dockware and NVIDIA. His work has appeared at venues including LoG, NeurIPS, EMNLP, ECCV, NLDL and INLG, and received a best paper award at ECCV 2026 OpenSUN3D. His earlier work was in evaluation, measuring what models can actually do, as opposed to what benchmarks credit them with, across language models and world models.

His research starts from the view that text is the narrowest channel in machine intelligence. Models think in high-dimensional internal state and must serialize that thinking into tokens, and people can reach a model only through that same channel. He works on removing the bottleneck, studying how learned systems form, keep, change and act on internal state, how much of a model’s thinking is lost when it must pass through text, and what a model can do when its reasoning stays in its own hidden states and is read there directly. His long-term aim is an intelligence in which people and models work as a single cognitive system, joining human creativity and judgment to machine memory, breadth and parallel inference to solve problems in science that neither could reach alone.
